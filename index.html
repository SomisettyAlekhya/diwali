Stories of Light

N
Northern India
उत्तर · Deepawali

The Return of Lord Rama

In Northern India, Diwali celebrates the triumphant return of Lord Rama, his wife Sita and his brother Lakshmana to the kingdom of Ayodhya after 14 years of exile and the defeat of the demon king Ravana. The people of Ayodhya lit rows of clay lamps — diyas — to guide them home through the dark night.

Welcome home, Shri Ram! 🙏

Southern India · Naraka Chaturdashi
தெற்கு · Deepavali

Lord Krishna's Victory over Narakasura

In Southern India, the festival is celebrated as Naraka Chaturdashi — the day Lord Krishna and his wife Satyabhama defeated the tyrannical demon Narakasura, freeing thousands of captives. Light triumphed over darkness, and the freed people celebrated with lamps.

Darkness defeated — the captives are free! ✨
